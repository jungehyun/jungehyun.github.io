Research

Mineral controls on soil carbon persistence

At KIST, building on a year in Dr. Eric Slessarev's group at Yale, I develop a mineral-informed soil carbon model that links reactive mineral phases (Fe and Al oxides, clay) to the turnover of soil organic carbon, evaluated against radiocarbon and fraction data (ISRaD) at global scale.

Measuring and verifying soil carbon change

Low-cost measurement–reporting–verification (MRV) for soil-based carbon dioxide removal (Sejong Science Fellowship, National Research Foundation of Korea, 2024–2028, PI), and national-scale assessment of how management changes soil carbon stocks with modified RothC models.

Soils under human influence

How land use and management reshape soil carbon and nitrogen: conservation tillage and biochar in croplands, and constructed urban soils and the ecosystem services they provide.
