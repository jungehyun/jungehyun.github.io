## 2. About (한 문단)
I am a soil scientist working on how soils store carbon and for how long. My current work at KIST builds process-based soil carbon models that use mineralogy (reactive Fe and Al phases, clay) to predict the persistence of soil organic carbon, building on a year at Yale University with Eric Slessarev. Before that, at Kyung Hee University, I worked on national-scale soil carbon assessment with modified RothC models, on low-cost measurement–reporting–verification (MRV) for soil-based carbon dioxide removal, and on urban soils and the ecosystem services they provide. I use process-based and data-driven models together with field and laboratory measurements of soil carbon fractions, greenhouse-gas fluxes and microbial properties.

## 3. Research (주제 3개, 각 2문장)
**Mineral controls on soil carbon persistence.** Developing a mineral-informed soil carbon model that links reactive mineral phases to the turnover of soil organic carbon, evaluated against radiocarbon and fraction data (ISRaD) at global scale. [Yale → KIST 현재 과제]
**Measuring and verifying soil carbon change.** Low-cost MRV approaches for soil-based carbon dioxide removal (Sejong Science Fellowship, National Research Foundation of Korea, 2024–2028, PI), national-scale assessment of management effects on soil carbon stocks with modified RothC, and Tier 3 methods for cropland soil carbon.
**Urban soils and ecosystem services.** Carbon forms and stability in urban and constructed soils, an ecosystem-services-based soil quality index for metropolitan areas, and CO2 flux monitoring in urban forests.
